Every call, step by step

  1. Checks the shop's unpaid bills from your books.
  2. Calls in the shop's language and introduces itself for you.
  3. Asks for a payment date and amount, and repeats it back.
  4. If the shop is in good standing, suggests items they usually reorder.
  5. Hands the call to you if they ask.
  6. Writes down the promise so you can see it on your dashboard.
