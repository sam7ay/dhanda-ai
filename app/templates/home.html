Questions

Good questions, simple answers

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. You upload one report from Busy or Marg, pick who to call, and press start. That's it.

Will my retailers know it's an AI?

Yes, if they ask, it says it's an assistant calling on your behalf. It's always polite and uses your business name.

Which languages does it speak?

Hindi and Punjabi. It starts in the language you set for each shop and switches if the shopkeeper does.

What if a retailer wants to talk to me?

The call is passed straight to your mobile. If you can't pick up, the assistant tells them you'll call back.

Can I try it before using my real data?

Yes. Try it with a sample business first, and make practice calls to your own phone.
